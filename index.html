About Me

I don't start with a tool. I start with the problem.

Someone usually arrives with a need, not a specification: customers who can't find a product, a gym that wants members to track their progress, a business that has to be seen. Before I open an editor, I want to know what is actually in the way.

Only then do I decide what to make. Sometimes the answer is a website. Sometimes it is an application. Sometimes it is a visual design, such as a thumbnail, a poster or a logo. Often it is a combination, and the pieces have to look like they belong together.

Technical Solution

I design and build responsive websites and applications, create the visual assets they need, and bring each piece together into a clear, practical experience.

  1. UnderstandWhat is the problem, who has it, and what does solving it look like?
  2. ChoosePick the digital solution that fits, instead of the one I happen to prefer.
  3. BuildDesign and develop it, check it on real screens, and put it online.

Website

Ashok Trading

Problem
A Mumbai wholesale supplier of tissue and disposable products sells across several brands, and customers need to find the right item and reach the shop directly.
Approach
The range is organised by company (Rossari, Kressa, ACE Paper & Tissue and others), then narrowed by product type. Call, message and map actions sit in a dedicated bulk-order block.
Features
Company-grouped product catalogue with type filters, an About page with dealership details, contact information and a Google Maps link.
Outcome
A live business website, designed and developed by me.
